/etc/xroad tree on ss.gerege.mn (snapshot 2026-05-08)

This SS additionally has the `xroad-securityserver-ee` country profile and
`xroad-opmonitor` operations-monitoring daemon, so you will see one extra
`override-securityserver-ee.ini`, one `op-monitor.ini` + logback, one
`opmonitor.conf` service file, and an `op-monitor` postgres database
compared to mgmt/rp.
